Before You Buy

  • Find out the platform. PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch or phone. A Switch game is useless to someone who only plays on PC.
  • Peripherals are the safest bet. Most gamers will happily replace a worn-out mouse, mouse pad or headset, and a spare is never wasted.
  • Avoid games they might own. If you are not sure, a gift card is the safe version of a game.
  • Keep the receipt. Gaming gear is personal. Amazon’s holiday return window usually gives extra time for gifts, but check the details on the listing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good gift for a gamer who has everything?

Something that wears out or gets upgraded often: a mouse pad, a new mouse, a better headset, or LED lights for their setup. A gift card for their main platform is also always useful.

Is a game a good gift?

Only if you know their platform and are fairly sure they don’t own it. Otherwise, a gift card for their platform lets them pick.

When is the best time to buy gaming gifts?

Black Friday and Cyber Monday usually have the biggest discounts on gaming accessories. Prices change from day to day, so check the listing before you buy.

Our Pick

If you don’t know what they play on, get the Razer BlackShark V2 X: it works with PC and consoles and almost every gamer uses a headset. For a PC player, the Logitech G305 is hard to go wrong with, and for a Switch player, Stardew Valley or an eShop gift card are safe choices.
