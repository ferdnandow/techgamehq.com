Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Welcome to TechGameHQ ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit techgamehq.com, why, and what choices you have. It is written to meet the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy rules on cookies.

Who We Are

TechGameHQ is an independent gaming and tech blog run from Ireland. For any privacy question or request, contact us at contact@techgamehq.com.

Information We Collect

You can read TechGameHQ without creating an account or giving us any personal details. We only receive personal information in these situations:

  • When you contact us. The form on our contact page is processed by Formspree, which forwards your name, email address, and message to our inbox. We use this only to reply to you.
  • When you accept analytics cookies. See "Cookies and Analytics" below.
  • When ads are shown. Google may collect information through advertising cookies. See "Advertising" below.

Like every website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) keeps technical server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and reliability. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

Cookies and Analytics

We do not set any analytics cookies unless you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you click Reject, the site works exactly the same and Google Analytics is never loaded. Advertising cookies are handled separately by Google’s own consent message, described under "Advertising" below.

If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which articles are read and how visitors find us. Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and collects information like pages visited, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type. We do not use it to identify you personally. Google acts as our data processor, and data may be transferred to the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your choice is stored in your own browser for six months. You can change it at any time with the Cookie Settings link at the bottom of every page. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Legal Basis

Analytics cookies are used only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Messages you send us are handled on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying to you. Server logs are kept by our host on the basis of legitimate interest in keeping the site secure.

How Long Data Is Kept

Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Messages sent through the contact form are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and then deleted.

Your Rights

If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email contact@techgamehq.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority — in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to delete it, and opt out of targeted advertising. Email us at contact@techgamehq.com. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Amazon Associates Disclosure

TechGameHQ is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When you click on links to Amazon products on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. This helps us keep the site running and producing free content. When you follow one of these links, Amazon may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense, an advertising service from Google, to show ads on TechGameHQ. Ads help pay for the site and keep the articles free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

If you choose not to receive personalised ads, you may still see ads, but they will be based on things like the page you are reading rather than your browsing history.

Visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent message from Google before any advertising cookies are set, and can change that choice later through the same message.

Visitors in US states with privacy laws (such as California) can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising through the privacy message Google shows on this site, or through the Google Ads Settings link above.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Our website contains links to third-party websites, including Amazon. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@techgamehq.com or through our contact page.
